## 🎓 Academic Context

This package was developed as part of a quantitative finance thesis:

**"Bellman Filter for Dynamic Factor Stochastic Volatility Models: Implementation and Empirical Analysis"**

The main package (this repository) provides a clean, professional implementation suitable for:
- ✅ Production use
- ✅ PyPI distribution  
- ✅ Academic citations
- ✅ Further research

The research repository preserves the complete academic journey and experimental work.
